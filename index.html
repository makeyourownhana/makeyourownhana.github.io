3 rules that stop the AI look

A real-looking photo isn't one that shows every pore. It's one a normal person would actually choose to post.

One real light

Window, sun or street lamp. Say where it is and which side of her face falls into shadow. Evenly lit faces give AI away.

Mid-movement

Catch her doing something: walking, tucking her hair, laughing at something off camera. Never script a stiff pose.

No eating

No biting, no drinking. AI mouths around food and cups still look wrong.
